Questions, answered

How often should I use it?
Around five minutes a day, morning or evening. Consistency matters more than session length, so it's designed to slot into a routine you'll actually keep.
When will I notice a difference?
Many people enjoy how their skin looks and feels straight after a session. Everyone's skin is different, so results vary and we don't promise a specific outcome. Regular use over several weeks gives you the best sense of what it does for you.
What do the three LED modes do?
Each light is designed for a different focus in your routine: red for a radiant look, blue for a clearer, balanced look, and purple for a calm, balanced finish. Switch between them as your skin needs.
Does the microcurrent hurt?
It shouldn't. Most people feel a light tingle. Start on the lowest intensity and only increase if it feels comfortable. Always use it with a serum or gel, never on dry skin.
Is it suitable for everyone?
Not everyone. Please don't use it if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device, have epilepsy, or have broken or irritated skin. Avoid the eye area. If you have a medical condition or are unsure, speak to your doctor before use.
