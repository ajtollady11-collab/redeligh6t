01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device when you visit it. They let the website remember information, such as what is in your shopping bag or that you are signed in.

Cookies set by the website you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by other companies, for example through adverts or embedded content, are called third-party cookies.

02What similar technologies are

Websites can also store or access information on your device in other ways, which UK law treats in the same way as cookies. These include:

  • Local storage, which keeps information in your browser until it is cleared;
  • Session storage, which keeps information only while a browser tab is open; and
  • Pixels and tags, small pieces of code often used for analytics or advertising. Our website does not use any.

In this policy, “cookies” includes these similar technologies.

03Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are essential for features you ask for, such as adding products to your bag or checking out. Under UK law, these do not need your consent. Everything our website currently stores falls into this category.

04Functional cookies

Functional cookies remember choices to make a website more convenient, beyond what is strictly needed. We do not currently use any functional cookies that require your consent. Remembering your region is only done when you choose a region yourself, as part of that feature.

05Analytics and performance cookies

Analytics cookies measure how visitors use a website, such as which pages are visited. Our website does not use any analytics or performance cookies or tools. We do not use Google Analytics or any similar service.

06Advertising and marketing cookies

Advertising cookies and pixels track visitors across websites to show them adverts or measure ad campaigns. Our website does not use any advertising or marketing cookies, pixels or tags, including those from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok or Google Ads.

07Payment-related technologies

When you continue to payment, you leave our website and go to a secure checkout page hosted by Square. Square may use cookies and similar technologies on its own pages, for example to process your payment securely and prevent fraud. These are set by Square, not by us, and are covered by Square’s own cookie and privacy policies.

Our website does not load Square’s scripts or cookies on our own pages.

08Third-party services

Our pages connect to these third-party services. None of them is used by us to set tracking cookies on our website:

  • Google Fonts: our typefaces are loaded from Google’s servers. Your browser sends your IP address and browser information to Google to receive the fonts. We do not use this for tracking.
  • Vercel: our website host. To show prices in your currency, our server reads the country your connection appears to come from, based on your IP address. Only the country is used, and nothing is stored on your device for this.
  • Square: our payment processor, as described in section 7.

Our server also looks up a public exchange rate to show US customers the amount charged in GBP. No information about you is sent for this.

09Your cookie preferences

Because our website only uses strictly necessary technologies, there are no optional cookies for you to switch on or off, and we do not show a cookie banner.

If we ever introduce optional cookies, such as analytics or advertising, we will not set them until you have agreed. Before that happens, we will add a cookie settings tool that lets you accept all, reject non-essential cookies, choose individual categories, and change your mind at any time.

10Controlling cookies in your browser

You can view, block and delete cookies and stored website data in your browser settings. Look for “Privacy”, “Cookies” or “Site data” in your browser’s settings or help pages. Most browsers also let you clear data for a single website.

If you block or delete the storage our website uses, you can still browse, but your bag will empty, your region choice will be forgotten, and anything typed into checkout will be cleared.

11Consent and withdrawing consent

We do not currently need your consent for any cookies, because we only use strictly necessary technologies. We never treat continuing to browse as consent.

If we introduce optional cookies in future, you will be asked to consent before they are used, you will be able to withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings tool on our website, and withdrawing will be as easy as giving consent.

12Personal information collected through cookies

The checkout information kept in your browser’s session storage can include personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. It stays on your device and is only sent to us when you continue to payment.

The dashboard sign-in cookie identifies a signed-in Aureli staff member’s session only. It does not contain customer information.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we use personal information and the rights you have.

13Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if we change the cookies or similar technologies we use, and before we introduce any optional cookies. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
