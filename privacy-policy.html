01Introduction and who we are

Aureli is an online skincare and beauty brand selling the Aureli™ Skin Therapy Wand and related accessories to customers in the United Kingdom and the United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices and rights you have.

Aureli is operated by a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. It is not a limited company. For the purposes of UK data protection law, the sole trader who runs Aureli is the controller of the personal information described in this policy, meaning they decide how and why it is used.

02Personal information we collect

We only collect the information we need to run our shop, deliver your order and look after you as a customer. Depending on how you use our website, this can include:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery details, such as your shipping address.
  • Order details, such as what you bought, how many, the price and the date.
  • Payment confirmation details provided to us by Square, such as whether a payment succeeded. We do not receive or store your full card details.
  • Communications, such as the emails you send us and our replies.
  • Technical information created when you use our website, described in section 5.

We do not ask for special category information (such as health information) when you buy from us. Please don’t include medical details in messages to us unless you need to, for example when asking whether our product is suitable for you.

03Contact, billing, delivery and order information

When you check out, you give us your email address, phone number, name and shipping address on our checkout page. We pass these details to Square so its secure payment page can be filled in for you, and Square collects your payment details directly.

Your order, including your name, contact details and delivery address, is stored in our Square account. We use it to pack and ship your order. We record fulfilment notes against the order in Square, such as whether it has been dispatched, the carrier, the tracking number and short internal notes. We do not keep a separate customer database on our own servers.

To make checkout easier, our website keeps a temporary copy of what you type into the checkout form in your own browser, so it isn’t lost if you go back to the shop. This copy stays on your device, is not sent to us until you continue to payment, and is cleared when you complete an order or close the browser tab. See our Cookie Policy for details.

04Payment processing through Square

Payments are processed by Square. When you continue to payment, you are taken to a secure checkout page hosted by Square, where you enter your card details or use Apple Pay or Google Pay.

  • Your card details go directly to Square. They are not entered on, stored on, or visible to the Aureli website.
  • Square shares with us the information we need to fulfil your order, such as your order, delivery details and whether the payment was successful.
  • Square also processes personal information for its own purposes, such as fraud prevention, security and meeting its legal obligations. When it does so, Square acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. You can read Square’s privacy notice on Square’s website.

US orders are charged in pounds sterling (GBP) by Square. To show you the amount you will pay, our server looks up a public exchange rate. No personal information is sent for this.

05Technical information collected through our website

Our website does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Some technical information is still processed automatically when you visit:

  • Hosting and server logs. Our website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel’s servers receive information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time, so they can deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • Approximate country. To show prices in pounds or dollars, our website asks our server which country your connection appears to come from, based on your IP address. Only the country is returned. We do not use this to identify you and we do not store it.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. To deliver the fonts, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. Google’s privacy policy applies to this.
  • Information stored in your browser. Our website stores a small amount of information in your browser to make the shop work, such as what is in your bag. See our Cookie Policy.

06How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • take payment for, process, pack and deliver your order;
  • send you information about your order and respond to your questions, requests and complaints;
  • handle cancellations, returns, refunds and claims about faulty products;
  • keep business, tax and accounting records;
  • keep our website, checkout and order records secure and prevent fraud or misuse;
  • run and improve our shop, for example by fixing problems you tell us about;
  • send marketing emails, but only where you have agreed to receive them (see section 12); and
  • comply with the law and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

07Our legal bases for using your information

UK data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for each way we use personal information. We rely on:

  • Contract: to process and deliver your order, take payment and deal with returns and refunds, because we need to do this to carry out our contract with you or take steps you ask for before buying.
  • Legal obligation: to keep tax and accounting records, meet consumer law duties (such as handling cancellations and faulty goods) and respond to lawful requests.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and secure our website, prevent fraud, answer general questions and improve our service. We only rely on this where our interests are not outweighed by your rights and interests.
  • Consent: to send marketing emails and for any optional cookies or similar technologies we may use in future. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

08Service providers and sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We also do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

We share personal information only where needed to run our business, with:

  • Square, which processes payments and stores order records for us, as explained in section 4.
  • Vercel, which hosts our website and the server functions that connect our checkout to Square.
  • Google, which serves the fonts used on our website (your IP address and browser information only).
  • Delivery companies, such as postal services and couriers, which receive your name, delivery address and, where needed for delivery, your phone number or email address.
  • Our email provider (Microsoft, for our Outlook mailbox), which processes emails you send us and our replies.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, such as accountants, HM Revenue & Customs, the police or courts, where we are required or permitted by law, or to protect our legal rights.

If the Aureli business is ever sold or transferred, customer information may be transferred to the new owner, who would have to use it in line with this policy.

09International data transfers

Some of the providers we use are based in, or process information in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. This includes Vercel, Google and Square’s group companies. If you are a customer in the United States, your information will also be processed in the UK, where Aureli is based.

Where personal information is transferred from the UK to another country, we rely on the protections provided by UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations, or the safeguards our providers put in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or certification under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified. You can contact us for more information about the safeguards that apply.

10How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, including meeting legal, tax and accounting requirements.

  • Order and transaction records: UK tax rules generally require sole traders to keep business records for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year. We keep order records for at least this long. We may keep them longer if we need them to deal with a claim or dispute.
  • Emails and customer service messages: kept for as long as needed to deal with your question or complaint and any follow-up, and for longer if they form part of our order records.
  • Information stored in your browser: stays on your device until it is cleared, as explained in our Cookie Policy.

Square, Vercel and Google keep information in line with their own retention policies. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

11Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses a small number of strictly necessary technologies, such as browser storage that remembers what is in your bag. It does not currently use analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, so we do not ask for cookie consent.

If we ever introduce optional cookies or similar technologies, we will ask for your consent first and update our Cookie Policy, which explains everything we use in detail.

12Marketing communications and opting out

We will only send you marketing emails, such as news about new products and offers, if you have agreed to receive them. Buying from us does not sign you up to marketing emails automatically.

At the time this policy was last updated, we do not use a third-party email marketing service. If we start using one, we will update this policy before we do so.

You can opt out of marketing at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by emailing aurelileds@outlook.com. We will still send you messages about your orders, as these are needed to fulfil them.

13Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal information. Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where there is no good reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances;
  • object to us using your information on the basis of legitimate interests, and to marketing at any time;
  • data portability, which means receiving information you gave us in a commonly used format, or asking us to send it to someone else, where this applies; and
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email aurelileds@outlook.com. We may need to ask for information to confirm your identity, such as the email address you ordered with. We will respond within one month, or tell you within that time if we need longer, as the law allows. Making a request is usually free.

Some rights have exceptions. For example, we may need to keep order records for tax purposes even if you ask us to delete them. If so, we will explain why.

14UK GDPR and US privacy rights

Customers in the United Kingdom

We process personal information in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Customers in the United States

Some US states have consumer privacy laws that give residents rights such as knowing what personal information a business collects, accessing it, correcting it, deleting it and opting out of its sale, of “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising, or of targeted advertising. Whether these laws apply to a business depends on factors such as its size and how much personal information it processes.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information using the contact details below, and we will respond in line with the law that applies to you. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising or use it for targeted advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (see section 16).

If you are a US resident and we decline a request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our response. Where your state’s law provides a right to appeal, we will handle your reply as an appeal.

15How we keep information secure

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • using an encrypted (HTTPS) connection across our website;
  • having card payments handled entirely by Square, so card details never pass through our website;
  • keeping access keys for Square on our server only, never in the website code your browser downloads;
  • protecting our order dashboard with a password and secure sign-in cookie; and
  • limiting access to customer information to what is needed to fulfil orders and provide support.

No website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the ICO where the law requires us to.

16Children’s privacy

Our products and website are intended for adults. You must be 18 or over to place an order. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and our website is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we change how we use personal information or start using a new service provider. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will make this clear on our website and, where appropriate, tell you by email.
